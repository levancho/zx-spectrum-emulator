Spectrum keyboard

On the 48K, one key types a whole keyword: at the start of a line P gives PRINT. Hold Ctrl or Alt for SYMBOL SHIFT (red), Shift for CAPS SHIFT. Punctuation, arrows and Backspace on your own keyboard work directly; Esc is BREAK.

Type your program normally, one numbered line per row, then press Run. Keywords can be upper or lower case; put a space after each keyword. Your program is kept in this browser between visits.
